Questions

Good to know.

Is BRAINROT RIFT free to play?

100% free. You can collect, fuse and climb the entire free battle pass without spending a single Robux. Premium cosmetics are optional.

What devices can I play on?

Anything that runs Roblox — mobile, tablet, PC, Mac and Xbox. Your progress syncs across all of them automatically.

How do fusions work?

Drop any three brainrots into the Fusion Lab. The combined rarity sets your odds — fusing three Legendaries gives the best shot at a Mythic, but even commons can surprise you.

Can my brainrots get stolen?

Only during Heist windows, and only if you leave them undefended. Keep your vault upgraded and your best rots are safe.

How often do you update?

New brainrots and a fresh rift event drop every weekend, with a full season reset roughly every six weeks.
